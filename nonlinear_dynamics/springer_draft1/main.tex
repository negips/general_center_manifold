\title[Extended Center Manifold]{Constructing asymptotic center manifold for extended systems}
\author*[]{\fnm{Prabal} S. \sur{Negi}}\email{prabal.negi@oist.jp}

%\author{Prabal S. Negi}
%\email[]{prabal.negi@oist.jp}
%\homepage[]{Your web page}
%\thanks{}
%\altaffiliation{}
\affil[]{Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology Graduate University, Onna, Okinawa 904-0495, Japan}

\date{\today}

\abstract{Center manifold approximation of a generic large nonlinear system at a multiple co-dimension bifurcation point is considered for cases where the system has been perturbed slightly away from the bifurcation point due to small parameter perturbations and harmonic forcing. We take the ``extended systems'' approach to analyze such systems which elevates parameter perturbation as well as harmonic forcing as state variables and extends the dimensionality of the center subspace. The new subspace structure is explicitly evaluated and analyzed in detail. Despite the extension of the operators, it is shown that the construction of an asymptotic center-manifold for such an extended system in fact only requires operations on the original linearized system at bifurcation. A key consequence of taking the ``extended systems'' perspective is that algorithms for parameter perturbation, harmonic forcing and unperturbed system center manifolds can be unified into a single algorithm. A Ginzburg-Landau model at bifurcation is used to demonstrate the validity of the approach based on the extended system.}
